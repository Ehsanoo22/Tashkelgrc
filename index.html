Fabrication Process: Engineering to Installation

Millimeter precision at every stage of architectural GFRC manufacturing in Damascus, Syria:

  1. 01. Design & Engineering

    Translating architectural concepts into detailed 3D BIM models and rigorous structural shop drawings. Every curve, anchor point, and joint tolerance is mathematically perfected before casting.

  2. 02. Mold Fabrication

    Utilizing high-precision 5-axis CNC milling to create custom rubber, wood, and fiberglass molds. This guarantees 0.5mm accuracy for even the most intricate parametric and organic geometries.

  3. 03. Casting & Curing

    Applying high-performance GFRC layers through specialized spray techniques. The alkali-resistant glass fibers are meticulously oriented for maximum tensile strength, impact resistance, and flawless surface finish.

  4. 04. On-Site Crane Installation

    Executing precision, crane-assisted mounting with concealed anchoring systems. Our specialized teams ensure structural alignment, seismic safety, and seamless joint caulking.

Why GFRC: Advanced Lightweight Architectural Concrete

Glass Fiber Reinforced Concrete (GRC / GFRC) is a revolution in architectural materiality, providing unmatched structural and aesthetic benefits:

  • 80% Lighter than Traditional Concrete

    Achieve the monumental visual depth of massive carved stone at a wall thickness under 20mm, drastically reducing dead loads, foundation costs, and structural steel framing.

  • Extreme Durability & 50+ Year Design Life

    Alkali-resistant glass fibers eliminate the need for ferrous rebar, preventing rust, concrete spalling, and cracking under Damascus weather fluctuations.

  • Limitless Architectural Freedom

    Cast complex double-curved panels, intricate Islamic mashrabiya lattices, and bespoke sculptural facades with unmatched surface fidelity.

Frequently Asked Questions on GRC & GFRC Architectural Facades

What is GFRC (Glass Fiber Reinforced Concrete)?

GFRC is a composite material made of Portland cement, fine aggregate, polymers, and alkali-resistant glass fibers. It offers high flexural and tensile strength with a fraction of the weight of standard precast concrete.

How durable is GFRC in Syrian weather conditions?

Tashkel GFRC is engineered for high thermal stability, non-combustibility (Class A1 fire rating), and resistance to moisture, UV exposure, and freeze-thaw cycles, with an expected lifespan exceeding 50 years.

Can Tashkel manufacture custom Islamic patterns and Mashrabiya screens?

Yes. Utilizing 5-axis CNC machining and 3D modeling, we craft bespoke geometric lattices, screens, and ornamental arabesque reliefs tailored to project specifications.
